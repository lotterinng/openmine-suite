# Code of Conduct

OpenMine Suite is built by surveyors, engineers, planners and developers who often disagree about
methods. That is healthy; how we disagree matters.

## Expected behaviour

* Be respectful and assume good faith. Critique code, data and methods — not people.
* Be precise. In an engineering tool, "it looks fine" is not evidence; tests, references and
  datasets are.
* Welcome newcomers, including people from operations who are new to open source.
* Respect confidentiality: never post a mine's survey data, plans or production figures without
  the owner's permission.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory language or imagery, personal attacks, threats, publishing others'
private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainers at the contact listed in `SECURITY.md` (mark the message
"Conduct"). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may warn, temporarily restrict, or
permanently remove participants whose behaviour violates this code. Maintainers who do not follow
the code may be removed from their role.
